# Celestial texture credits

- Earth day map: Solar System Scope, [source and authorship](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Solarsystemscope_texture_2k_earth_daymap.jpg), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The image was used unchanged as a texture on a new 3D sphere. It is based on NASA data with some adjusted colours and filled terrain.
- Mars map: Solar System Scope, [source and authorship](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Solarsystemscope_texture_2k_mars.jpg), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The image was used unchanged as a texture on a new 3D sphere. It is based on NASA data with some adjusted colours and filled terrain.
- Moon map: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, Ernie Wright, [CGI Moon Kit](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/), 2025 colour map. Used unchanged as a texture on a new 3D sphere; NASA notes the map is optimized for visual presentation rather than scientific measurement.

Retain these credits and license links in any game distribution using the Earth or Mars textures. No creator or NASA endorsement is implied.
